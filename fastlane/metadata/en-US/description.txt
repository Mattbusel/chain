Chain is the habit tracker that takes five seconds a day and makes your progress impossible to ignore. Tick a habit off, watch the ring fill, and keep the chain going. Every habit gets a year of squares stitched like a quilt: colour where you did it, a hole where you didn't.

NEW IN 1.3
• A challenge every month: one habit, one month, four days in five wins the badge. This month's challenge is open to everyone
• Milestone moments at 7, 30, 100 and 365 days, with an image to share
• Insights: this month against last, the habits that lift each other, your best time of day and when each chain hits its next milestone
• Log Next Habit in Control Center, on the Lock Screen and on the Action button
• A Best Chain widget that looks great in StandBy, and a Lock Screen line under the clock
• Ask Siri "How's my chain?"

TICK IT OFF FROM ANYWHERE
• Home Screen widgets you can tap: tick a habit off without opening the app
• Lock Screen ring, line and inline count
• "Log Read in Chain" with Siri, or the Log a habit shortcut
• A reminder with a Done button that stays quiet on days you've already done it

HABITS THAT FIT REAL LIFE
• Every day, certain weekdays, or a number of times a week
• Amounts and targets: 8 glasses, 20 pages, 10,000 steps, filled in tap by tap
• Apple Health ticks off steps, exercise, mindful minutes and workouts for you
• Quit habits: count the days clean, log a slip, keep your best on record
• Morning, afternoon and evening groups, so the day reads top to bottom

MISSED A DAY? THE CHAIN CAN SURVIVE IT
• Freezes skip a sick day without breaking the chain
• Pause a habit for a holiday and pick it straight back up
• Streak Repair patches a missed day in gold when the freezes run out

SEE YOUR YEAR
• Week grid to log or fix any day
• The year quilt for every habit, with totals, best chain and completion
• Stats on your best and worst weekdays, perfect days and longest chains
• Notes on any day, and a shareable image of your chain
