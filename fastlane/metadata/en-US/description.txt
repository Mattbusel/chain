Chain is a habit tracker that takes five seconds a day and makes the pattern impossible to ignore.

Tick the habit, watch the ring fill, and keep the chain. Every habit gets a year of squares stitched like a quilt: lime where you did it, a hole where you did not.

TODAY
• Big tactile tiles, one tap to log, a completion ring for the day
• A fourteen-day strip under each habit and the current streak beside it

HABITS THAT FIT REAL LIFE
• Every day, certain weekdays, or a number of times a week
• Weekly habits count in weeks, not days, so a Tuesday off does not break a three-a-week chain
• Twenty icons, start dates, an optional daily reminder

WEEK
• A grid of the week to log or undo any day, faded where a habit is not due

YEAR
• Twenty-six weeks of squares per habit, with totals, best chain and the completion rate

STATS
• Check-ins all time, 30-day completion, longest chain, perfect days, and which weekdays you actually do the work

No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone.
